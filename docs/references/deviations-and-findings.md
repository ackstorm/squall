# Deviations and findings ledger
Running list of everything found during implementation that deviates from the spec, invents
something the spec does not cover, or is a known hazard we chose not to fix yet.
**Purpose:** we do NOT stop to adjudicate these one at a time — that costs pace. They are
logged here and reviewed in one pass with the architecture agent at the end of v0.1.
**Status values:** `OPEN` (needs a decision) · `ACCEPTED` (deliberate, documented) ·
`CLOSED` (resolved; kept for the record).
**How to use this file:** every implementer and reviewer appends. New entries go at the bottom
of their section with the next free ID. Never renumber. Never delete an entry — move it to
`CLOSED` with the resolution.

**Commit hashes below predate v0.1.0.** This repository's history starts at a single `initial
v0.1.0` commit; the 224-commit development history that these entries cite was squashed into
it at release time and preserved out-of-band as a git bundle. A short hash here identifies a
change in that archive, not a commit you can `git show`.
